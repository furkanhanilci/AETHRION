# Document Archetypes

**IMRaD is one archetype, not the default.** Structure is selected from the
reader's decision, the evidence topology and the delivery context.

| Archetype | Primary reader · their decision | Evidence burden | Typical topology |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Journal article** | Reviewer, then field · is this sound and new? | Highest; every claim cited or measured | IMRaD, venue-shaped |
| **Conference paper** | Reviewer under a page limit · accept? | High, compressed | IMRaD with a hard budget |
| **Thesis / chapter** | Committee · has the candidate demonstrated competence? | High, and **pedagogical** — method is explained, not just reported | Context → theory → method → studies → synthesis |
| **Literature review** | Field · what is known and what is missing? | Every synthesis statement resolves to a source set | Thematic · methodological · contradiction-and-gap |
| **Systematic review** | Field · what does the whole evidence base say? | PRISMA contract binds | Protocol → search → screen → synthesis → certainty |
| **Experimental report** | Team · did it work, and what next? | Result-linked; runs cited | Question → setup → run → result → interpretation |
| **Industrial R&D report** | Manager, engineer, customer · what do we do? | Requirement- and test-linked | Problem → requirements → method → V&V → recommendation |
| **Design / architecture report** | Engineering · is this the right structure? | Decision-linked, alternatives shown | Problem → alternatives → trade-offs → decision → risks |
| **V&V report** | Assurance, customer · does it meet requirements? | Every claim traces to a test result | Requirement → method → result → deviation → verdict |
| **White paper** | Informed non-specialist · should we care? | Lower density, **same honesty** | Context → problem → approach → implication |
| **Research proposal** | Funder, committee · fund this? | Planned work is **not** written as done | Gap → question → method → feasibility → plan |
| **Progress report** | Sponsor · continue, adjust, stop? | Status- and evidence-linked | Plan → actual → variance → risk → ask |
| **Technical memo** | One team · a narrow answer | Focused | Question → finding → implication |
| **Executive brief** | Decision-maker with minutes · decide | Highest **compression**, no loss of qualification | Decision → basis → risk → recommendation |
| **Supplement / appendix** | Verifier · can I check this? | Completeness over narrative | Mirrors the parent document |

## Failure modes by archetype

| Archetype | The characteristic failure |
|---|---|
| Journal article | Contribution inflated to clear a novelty bar |
| Conference paper | Method compressed until it is unreproducible |
| Thesis | A survey with no argument of its own |
| Literature review | Paper-by-paper summary instead of synthesis |
| R&D report | Recommendation not traceable to any measurement |
| V&V report | A deviation quietly reworded as a limitation |
| White paper | Qualification dropped for readability |
| **Proposal** | **Planned work described in the past tense** |
| Executive brief | Compression that removes the condition the result depends on |

## Selecting

```
Who reads it, and what do they decide?
        ↓
What evidence exists, and how is it shaped?
        ↓
Is there a venue, customer or institutional template?
        ↓
Which archetype fits — and does the evidence topology fit its topology?
```

If the archetype and the evidence disagree, **the evidence wins** and the
archetype changes. Forcing IMRaD onto a decision memo produces a document whose
structure fights its purpose.
