## Rule of use

Files under `70 - Literature Sets/Zotero Sources` are regenerated by the Bridge
API and are never edited by hand. Human analysis lives in `20 - Source Notes`,
curated sets at the root of `70 - Literature Sets`, and decisions and synthesis
in the corresponding numbered human areas.
